HYPER·CAR — a fictional Le Mans Hypercar built entirely in code: the body is a lofted ring-inset surface from analytic design curves, the livery is painted on canvases and projected in a custom shader, the 2×2 twill carbon is procedural with an anisotropy map.

PROGRESSIVE RENDER — when the camera is still, frames are re-rendered with sub-pixel jitter and a jittered area key light and averaged (64 samples) for clean AA and soft shadows.
NIGHT TRACK — wet asphalt with planar reflections, sodium track lights, headlight beams, motion streaks, light trails. Brake discs glow when braking (press B).
KEYS — 1–7 cameras · 0 reset · N mode · X x-ray · A aero · L lights · B brake · C livery
